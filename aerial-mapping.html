Understand Your Site from Above

Traditional ground-level photographs can make it difficult to capture the full layout and condition of a large site.

Lothian Drones uses professional drone technology to capture detailed aerial imagery, providing clients with a clear overview of their property, development or operational site.

Depending on project requirements, overlapping aerial photographs can be processed to produce high-resolution two-dimensional maps, giving clients a useful visual reference for planning, documentation and progress monitoring.

Our Aerial Mapping Services

2D Aerial Mapping

Capture overlapping aerial photographs for processing into detailed orthomosaic maps that provide a comprehensive overhead view of a site.

Visual Site Surveys

Aerial photography to document site layouts, access routes, structures, surrounding land and visible site conditions.

Construction Site Documentation

Record the condition and layout of a development site at different stages, creating a consistent visual record as work progresses.

Land & Property Overviews

High-quality aerial imagery of commercial premises, estates, land and property portfolios to support visual assessments and project discussions.

Repeatable Site Monitoring

Drone technology makes it possible to revisit a site and capture aerial imagery from planned flight routes over time.

This can help clients compare different stages of a project, monitor visible changes and maintain a photographic record of site development.

Whether you require a single aerial overview or scheduled site visits, we can discuss a service tailored to your project.
