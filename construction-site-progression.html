Construction Site Progression

Professional drone photography and aerial monitoring to document construction projects from initial groundwork through to completion.

A Clearer View of Your Construction Project

Lothian Drones provides professional aerial photography and construction site monitoring services throughout Scotland.

Our drone technology allows developers, construction companies and project managers to monitor progress, document milestones and maintain a visual record of their projects.

From residential developments to commercial construction projects, our aerial imagery provides a valuable perspective that traditional ground-level photography cannot achieve.

Regular Construction Progress Monitoring

We offer scheduled drone visits throughout the construction process, allowing clients to maintain a consistent photographic record of site development.

Whether you require weekly, fortnightly or monthly updates, our services can be tailored to your project.

Regular aerial photography helps demonstrate progress, identify visible changes and provide valuable updates to stakeholders.

Construction Site Photography

Our high-resolution aerial photography captures detailed images of construction sites from multiple perspectives.

These images can be used for project documentation, management reports, stakeholder presentations and promotional material.

2D Aerial Mapping

Lothian Drones can provide aerial imagery suitable for creating detailed 2D maps of construction sites.

These maps provide a useful overview of site layouts, development progress and changes throughout construction.

Mapping services are subject to site conditions, flight permissions and project requirements.
