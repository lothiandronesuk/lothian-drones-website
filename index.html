AI-Powered Roof Inspections

Discover a smarter approach to roof inspections with Lothian Drones.

Our drone inspection service combines detailed aerial imagery with specialised artificial intelligence software designed specifically to analyse roof conditions and identify potential defects.

By inspecting roofs remotely using drone technology, we can reduce the need for ladders, scaffolding and physical access to potentially hazardous roof areas during the initial inspection process.

Our AI-assisted reports highlight potential issues detected in the imagery, providing homeowners, property managers and commercial clients with useful information about the condition of their roofs.

Reports can also be shared with roofing contractors to support further investigation or repair quotations.

Suitable for: Residential properties, commercial buildings, property portfolios, housing organisations and local authorities.
